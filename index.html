About Ultimate Hostel

Ultimate Hostel is situated at Bomso and is committed to providing a very conducive and affordable accommodation to students of Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology and beyond. Our Hostel facilities meet the University accommodation standards and have moderate room prices with easy access to KNUST campus at any given time on foot. The hostel has 24-hour protection for students since Police Station is located besides it, to check safety, comfort and satisfaction of students.
